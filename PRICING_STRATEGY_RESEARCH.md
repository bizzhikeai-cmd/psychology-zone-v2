## 🧠 Consumer Psychology Principles

### 1. Anchoring Effect
**What it is**: The first price people see becomes their reference point.

**Application**: 
- Show single session price (₹750) prominently first
- Then show package price (₹1,499 for 3) - feels like a deal
- "Was ₹2,250, Now ₹1,499" creates strong anchor

### 2. Decoy Effect (Asymmetric Dominance)
**What it is**: Adding a third, less attractive option makes the target option look better.

**Example:**
```
Option A: 1 session  = ₹750     (anchor)
Option B: 3 sessions = ₹1,499   ← TARGET (₹499/session)
Option C: 4 sessions = ₹2,199   ← DECOY (₹549/session - worse than B!)
```
Option C makes Option B look amazing because you get almost the same per-session rate with less commitment.

### 3. Price-Quality Association
**What it is**: In services like therapy, too-low prices signal poor quality.

**Application**:
- ₹750 base price is good - not cheap, not premium
- Positions Psychology Zone as "affordable quality"
- BetterLYF at ₹849-999 is premium tier
- Below ₹500 signals "chatbot" or inexperienced therapists

### 4. Commitment & Consistency
**What it is**: Once people commit, they want to stay consistent with that commitment.

**Application**:
- Multi-session packages increase follow-through
- "Wellness Journey" framing (not just "sessions")
- Progress tracking encourages completion
- 70% of therapy clients become repeat clients

### 5. Loss Aversion
**What it is**: People feel losses 2x more intensely than equivalent gains.

**Application**:
- "Save ₹751" is more powerful than "Get 3 sessions for ₹1,499"
- "Your ₹50 discount expires in 24 hours"
- "Don't lose your reserved slot"

### 6. The Rule of 3
**What it is**: People prefer choosing from 3 options (not 2, not 5).

**Application**:
- Offer exactly 3 packages
- Middle option is usually chosen most (make it your target)

## 💰 Recommended Pricing Structure

### Option A: Simple 3-Tier (Recommended to Start)

| Package | Sessions | Price | Per Session | You Save | vs. Base |
|---------|----------|-------|-------------|----------|----------|
| **Single Session** | 1 | ₹750 | ₹750 | - | Anchor |
| **Starter Pack** ⭐ | 3 | ₹1,499 | ₹499 | ₹751 | **33% off** |
| **Wellness Journey** | 6 | ₹2,499 | ₹416 | ₹2,001 | **44% off** |

**Why this works:**
- Single session for commitment-phobes
- 3-pack is sweet spot (most will buy this)
- 6-pack for serious clients + maximum savings
- Clear savings at each tier

---

### Option B: 4-Tier with Decoy Effect (Advanced)

| Package | Sessions | Price | Per Session | You Save |
|---------|----------|-------|-------------|----------|
| Single Session | 1 | ₹750 | ₹750 | - |
| **Starter Pack** | 3 | ₹1,499 | ₹499 | ₹751 |
| Growth Pack | 4 | ₹2,199 | ₹549 | ₹801 |
| **Transformation** | 6 | ₹2,499 | ₹416 | ₹2,001 |

**The trick:** 4-session pack at ₹2,199 makes 6-session pack irresistible (only ₹300 more for 2 extra sessions!)

## ❓ Questions for Brand Owner

### Pricing Decisions:

1. **Base Price Confirmation**
   - Current site shows ₹599
   - Proposed new base: ₹750
   - Is ₹750 acceptable? Or stay at ₹599?

2. **Package Tiers**
   - Option A (3 tiers) or Option B (4 tiers with decoy)?
   - 3-pack at ₹1,499 = ₹499/session (33% off) - confirm?
   - 6-pack at ₹2,499 = ₹416/session (44% off) - confirm?

3. **Package Naming**
   - Professional: "Starter Pack" / "Growth Pack" / "Transformation"
   - Simple: "3 Sessions" / "6 Sessions"
   - Wellness-focused: "Begin" / "Grow" / "Thrive"

### Operational Decisions:

4. **Validity Period**
   - Should packages expire? (e.g., "Use within 90 days")
   - Recommendation: Yes, 90-day validity
   - Creates urgency, prevents indefinite deferrals

5. **Scheduling Flexibility**
   - Book all sessions upfront?
   - Or flexible "credits" system?
   - Recommendation: Credits system (more appealing)

6. **Transferability**
   - Can unused sessions be gifted/transferred?
   - Recommendation: No (prevents abuse)

7. **Refund Policy for Packages**
   - Full refund if no sessions used?
   - Partial refund (deduct used sessions at single rate)?
   - No refunds?

### Marketing Decisions:

8. **Discount Strategy**
   - Abandoned cart: ₹50 off first session?
   - Post-session upsell: ₹100 off next booking?
   - First-time package discount?

9. **Coupon Codes**
   - FIRST50 (₹50 off first session)
   - WELLNESS100 (₹100 off packages)
   - RETURN50 (₹50 off for returning clients)

10. **Launch Strategy**
    - Soft launch with select users?
    - Hard switch on specific date?
    - A/B test old vs new pricing?
